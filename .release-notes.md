### What's in 2.6.0

Your boards sync with your account. Each device that you sign in to shows the same boards, also the Android app.

- **Board sync**  - The name, note, vehicle profile and saved parameters of each board go to your account. Your other devices show them. Assistant chats stay on the device where you made them.
- **Boards from the cloud**  - A board that you did not connect on this device shows in the picker as "From the cloud". You can read its note and parameters here. You cannot change them here.
- **One board on all devices**  - The app knows a board by its hardware ID. A board that you connect on a phone and then on a computer is one board. When two devices change the same note, the later change stays.
- **Cloud line**  - Each board shows if it is in your account. You can sync now, remove the board from your account, or turn board sync off. When you sign out, the boards from your account go from this device.
- **Offline**  - Without a network, the app shows the boards and parameters that it got last time.
- **Safety**  - Sync does not write parameters to a vehicle. It only keeps and shows copies.
