### What's in 2.7.0

PID review measures how the rate loops respond. On a phone, the board on the link opens as a full board screen.

- **PID review**  - The app estimates the response of each rate loop from the whole flight. It gives the step response, the bandwidth, and the phase and gain margins when the log has the data. It reads copter, quadplane VTOL and fixed-wing loops. It shows the logging setup of the log and the gaps in it, so a log written too slowly or with gaps is clear. It finds the System ID runs in the log, so each can be measured on its own.
- **Log findings**  - Each log on this computer is checked once by HardwareReport, FilterReview, PIDReview, MAGFit and the motor balance, in the background and without an assistant request. The Logs tab shows the findings with the flags of the log. A click opens a window with the findings and the numbers of each tool. You can move and resize this window, and open it in a separate window. A button gives them to the assistant with the log, so the assistant does not run the same tools again. The column headers of the Logs tab align with the rows again.
- **Loops in windows**  - The map, the loop and the scope of the Loops tab can open in a window. The window stays when you open another tab, and it can move to its own window on another monitor. All copies show the same card and axis.
- **Response charts**  - The assistant can draw the step response or the Bode chart of the rate loops under its reply.
- **Log reports**  - The hardware report reads the GPS fix, the EKF failsafe, the flight modes and the board voltage of ArduPilot 4.x logs. A value that the log does not have shows as empty, not as zero. The report fails a flight where the vehicle did not follow its desired attitude, also when the crash check did not fire. EKF3's first yaw alignment after takeoff is no longer a warning. The report and the motor balance say what each number means.
- **Filter review**  - The review reads the filtered gyro capture from the log. It shows when the notch is not on the motor line, and then it gives a new notch frequency and reference throttle.
- **Faster log analysis**  - The log tools read only the messages that they need.
- **Phone: board screen**  - The board on the link opens with its parameters, logs and assistant chats. Each log shows as a two-line card. The parameter actions are in a ⋯ menu.
- **Flight modes**  - The modes screen takes the list of modes from the ArduPilot documentation, so every mode, also SystemID, shows by name. Parameter descriptions now show for vehicle parameters such as FLTMODE1.
- **Reboot needed**  - A dot on the board menu shows that the board needs a reboot. The menu says why: a parameter that ArduPilot reads at boot changed, the list of logs is wrong, or the board asked for it. The dot goes after the reboot.
- **Log downloads**  - A log streams to its end, and lost parts are asked for again at once. The Logs tab shows the download speed and the time left; on a slow radio or Wi-Fi link it says that a USB cable is faster. A downloaded log shows as on this computer also when the board lists another size for it. The Logs menu can set a new log for each flight.
- **Link chip**  - The header names the vehicle on the link by its board name.
- **Board log storage**  - The Logs tab shows when the board's log storage is full and new flights are not recorded, and when the board's list of logs is wrong and a reboot is needed.
- **Stability**  - When a tool stops on an internal error, the assistant gets an error and continues the reply.
