### What's in 2.5.0

Log downloads and gyro filter analysis work better. You can view boards that are not connected. Setup is easier to read.

- **Log downloads**  - Downloads over a slow link no longer hang. The app asks again for the parts that are missing, and other telemetry slows down while a log downloads.
- **Log list**  - Each log shows its board, date and size. SITL and imported logs have their own tags and folders.
- **Board identity**  - A board is known by its own hardware ID, not by "system 1". Its name and notes stay with it.
- **Boards without a link**  - Open any board you used before from the picker. You can see its logs, parameters and chats. The assistant can also read other boards.
- **Gyro spectrum**  - The spectrum and spectrogram use the WebTools method and its dB scale. The filter response is on the right axis. Harmonics are marked as notched or not notched.
- **Filter advice**  - The assistant simulates the notch filter for each batch, as in WebTools FilterReview. It suggests only the changes that remove more noise with less delay.
- **Assistant starters**  - Shorter buttons for pre-flight checks, tuning and filter review. A button puts its text in the box, so you can change it before you send.
- **Setup**  - A compass that is turned off and a plane without an airspeed sensor show grey, not as a problem. Step names all start with a capital letter. The group counters were removed.
- **Battery**  - The thresholds use the chemistry from the vehicle profile. A Li-ion pack can go to 3.0 V a cell. The battery drawing shows the cells, chemistry and capacity.
- **Radio channels**  - Choose the function of each channel from 5 to 16, or move the flight mode switch. The names are the same as in the firmware and Mission Planner.
- **Motor RPM**  - With ESC telemetry, each motor output shows its RPM.
- **Flight display**  - The display shows the flight mode and when the vehicle is armed. Under the height it shows the mission waypoint in Auto, or the distance to home.
- **MAVLink 0.21**  - The link library is updated.
