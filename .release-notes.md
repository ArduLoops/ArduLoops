### What's in 2.10.0

The Mission tab now plans full missions: point types, actions and conditions. The map shows them, and during the flight it shows what the vehicle does.

- **Point types**  - A point can be a waypoint, a loiter for a time, a loiter for a number of turns, or a landing. After a landing point, the mission does not add RTL.
- **Actions and conditions**  - Each point has a list of actions: speed, servo, relay, camera trigger by distance and jump to a point. A copter can look at a point (ROI). A plane can mark the start of the landing. Conditions wait for a time, a distance or a heading, and hold the actions after them.
- **Mission on the map**  - The map shows action badges, the jump arrow, the ROI line, loiter circles at their real radius and the landing point.
- **Mission in flight**  - In AUTO, done actions turn green. An action that waits shows the time or distance that is left. The log shows each action as it starts. The L1 line follows takeoff, jumps, loiters and RTL as ArduPilot flies them.
- **Read from the board**  - The app reads the mission from the board after the link starts and after an upload. It shows if the plan and the board are the same. One click loads the board mission into the plan.
- **Map layers**  - Select the grid, the street map, the satellite or the topographic map. You can zoom out to the whole world.
- **Logs**  - Bytes inside a log text do not change how the app reads other log messages.
